# Blind Guides Rules

## Setup

See [Variations](#variations) for alternate rule options, like starting with a different number of [Pointer tokens](#pointer-tokens) or using special colors.

In a standard game, start with eight [Pointer tokens](#pointer-tokens) and zero [Stumble tokens](#stumble-tokens).

In a standard game, use Red, Yellow, Green, Blue, and White cards. In each of those colors, there are three 1s, two 2s, two 3s, two 4s, and two 5s.

Determine player order, which remains fixed throughout the game.

*Note: player order should be determined before cards are dealt so that players can't decide who goes first based on what they see in someone else's hand.*

Shuffle the deck, and deal five cards to each player. Players may never view the cards in their own hand, but they may always view the cards in other players' hands.

### Pointer tokens

Pointer tokens are spent to [give clues](#give-a-clue).

In a standard game, the team begins with eight Pointer tokens.

Spent Pointer tokens can typically be restored by [discarding a card](#discard-a-card) or by [completing a color](#attempt-to-play-a-card).

The team may never exceed the staring number of Pointer tokens, but players may still perform actions that would normally earn a Pointer token even if they already have the starting number of Pointer tokens. In this case, simply skip the step in which the team earns a Pointer token.

### Stumble tokens

Stumble tokens are typically gained for a [misplay](#attempt-to-play-a-card).

If the team reaches the stumble-token threshold (three in a standard game), then the [team loses](#game-end).

## Communication rules

"Table talk" is not allowed. I.e., except when explicitly allowed by the rules (like when [Giving a clue](#give-a-clue)), players may not give any indications of what cards other players have, what they want other players to do, etc.

Players may decide on conventions *before* the game, like "a number clue means play" and "a color clue means hold", but these conventions are not binding and cannot be discussed during the game.

Players may take private notes.

## Taking a turn

On your turn, you may take one action, and then play passes to the next player (unless the game ends). There are three options for the action:
* [Give a clue](#give-a-clue)
* [Discard a card](#discard-a-card)
* [Attempt to play a card](#attempt-to-play-a-card)

### Give a clue
If the team has no remaining [Pointer tokens](#pointer-tokens), then this action cannot be taken.

To perform this action, take the following steps:
1. Spend one [Pointer token](#pointer-tokens).
1. Choose another player.
1. Choose a color or a number that matches at least one of that player's cards, and tell that player *all* of their matching cards.

### Discard a card

To perform this action, take the following steps:
1. Discard a card from your hand and reveal it. That card goes to the discard pile and will no longer be playable.
1. Gain a [Pointer token](#pointer-tokens), unless the team already has the starting number of [Pointer tokens](#pointer-tokens) (eight in a standard game).
1. Draw a card from the deck, unless there are no cards left in the deck.

### Attempt to play a card

For a standard color, cards are played in strictly increasing order. That is, a card in a standard color is playable if and only if both of the following criteria hold:
1. The number of the card is not yet on the played pile for this color.
1. All lower numbers are already on the played pile for this color.

Playing a card completes a color if it results in one copy of each number for that color being on the played pile.

To perform this action, take the following steps:
1. Select a card from your hand and reveal it.
   * If it is playable, then it goes to the played pile. If it completes a color, then the team gains one [Pointer token](#pointer-tokens), unless the team already has the starting number of [Pointer tokens](#pointer-tokens) (eight in a standard game).
   * If it is not playable (this is referred to as a misplay), then it goes to the discard pile, and the team gains one [Stumble token](#stumble-tokens).
1. Draw a card from the deck, unless there are no cards left in the deck.


## Game end

The game ends when at least one of the following conditions holds:
1. The team has reached the threshold of [Stumble tokens](#stumble-tokens) (3 tokens in a standard game). The team LOSES in this case.
1. It is no longer possible to complete all the colors. This happens when all copies of a particular number-color combination are in the discard pile. The team LOSES in this case.
1. All colors are complete. The team WINS in this case.

### Empty deck

If the deck runs out of cards, don't draw cards when you normally would do so. Otherwise, continue playing as usual. In particular, you may still perform actions that would normally result in drawing a card, i.e., [discarding](#discard-a-card) or [playing](#attempt-to-play-a-card).

*Note: An empty deck does not trigger the end of the game.*

## Variations

Before the start of the game, the team (or the host) may decide on a variety of non-standard rules. For example, they may decide that players will have six cards in their hands instead of five, that the threshold for [Stumble tokens](#stumble-tokens) is five instead of three, that the number of actions per turn is two instead of one, etc.

They may also play with color-specific rules. For example, some color might match every color clue, some color might match no color clues, some color might be played in decreasing order instead of increasing order, some color might earn a [Pointer token](#pointer-tokens) for a misplay and a [Stumble token](#stumble-tokens) for a discard, etc.
